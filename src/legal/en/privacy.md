In compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data (GDPR), and the Spanish Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on the Protection of Personal Data and guarantee of digital rights (LOPDGDD), we inform you about the processing of the personal data you provide to us through the website `perito.barcelona`.

### 1. Data Controller

* **Owner:** Albert Vilardell Serra
* **Professional Address:** Carrer Numància, 95, Local 5, Distrito de Les Corts, 08029 Barcelona, Spain
* **Email:** info@perito.barcelona
* **Website:** https://perito.barcelona

### 2. Purpose of Data Processing

Your personal data will be processed for the following purposes, depending on the reason you have provided them to us:

1.  **Contact Form and Email Communications:** To manage and respond to requests for information, inquiries, service proposals, or any type of request made by the user through the contact means made available.
2.  **Management of the Contractual Relationship:** In the event of contracting our services, for administrative management, invoicing, and compliance with the contractual and legal obligations arising from the provision of said expert, audit, or consulting services.
3.  **Cookies:** This website may use technical and analytical cookies to improve the user's browsing experience.

### 3. Legal Basis for Data Processing

* For handling inquiries and information requests, the legal basis is your **express consent**, given by checking the acceptance box and submitting the form (Article 6.1.a of the GDPR).
* For the provision of contracted services, the legal basis is the **performance of a contract** for the provision of services and compliance with applicable legal obligations (Article 6.1.b and 6.1.c of the GDPR).

### 4. Data Retention Periods

* Data collected through the contact form will be kept as long as its deletion is not requested by the data subject.
* In the case of clients, the data will be kept for the duration of the contractual relationship and, subsequently, for the periods legally established for compliance with tax and legal obligations (generally, a minimum of 6 years).

### 5. Data Recipients

Your personal data will not be transferred to third parties, unless there is a legal obligation to do so.

No international data transfers will be made to countries outside the European Economic Area.

### 6. Rights of the Data Subject

You have the right to exercise your rights of **Access, Rectification, Erasure, Restriction of processing, Portability, and Objection** regarding your personal data at any time.

To exercise these rights, you can send a written communication to the email address `info@perito.barcelona`, clearly indicating the right you wish to exercise and attaching a copy of your ID card or equivalent identification document.

Furthermore, if you believe your rights have been violated, you have the right to file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

### 7. Security Measures

The Data Controller has adopted the necessary technical and organizational measures to ensure the security of personal data and to prevent its alteration, loss, or unauthorized access or processing.

### 8. Changes to the Privacy Policy

The Data Controller reserves the right to modify this policy to adapt it to new legislation or jurisprudence. In such cases, the changes introduced will be announced on this page with reasonable advance notice of their implementation.
