## Our Role: Engineers at the Service of Your Defense

As **construction expert witnesses in Barcelona**, our work goes beyond simple inspection. We act as the link between engineering and law, translating complex construction pathologies into understandable and solid technical arguments.

A **construction expert report** is the fundamental tool when conflict arises. At **Perito Barcelona**, we cover all phases of the construction cycle and its possible pathologies:

### Problems We Solve: Hidden Defects and Pathologies

* **Party and Judicial Reports:** We prepare the necessary technical evidence to support your lawsuit or defense.
* **Hidden Defects Inspection:** If you have bought a home with dampness problems, cracks, or deficient installations, we document the damage for your claim.
* **Quality Control and Audit:** We verify that what is built complies with the project and current regulations (*Lex Artis*).
* **Reports for Claims:** Analysis of causes in fires, floods, or structural collapses.

### Our Technical Authority: Chartered Civil Engineers

Not all experts have the same solvency. Our training as **Civil Engineers** allows us to analyze structure and foundation with a level of detail that other technicians do not reach. In addition, our experience in judicial ratification in the courts of Barcelona guarantees that the report will be defended forcefully before the judge.

> "A good expert witness not only finds the error but explains why it happened and how much it costs to fix it."
