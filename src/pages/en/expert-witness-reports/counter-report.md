## The Importance of Auditing Opposing Evidence

Accepting the opposing party's or court expert's report (if it contains errors) without questioning is a risk you should not take. Many opinions contain flaws that may go unnoticed by a lawyer or judge but are evident to another expert engineer.

Our **opinion critique** work consists of subjecting the report to a rigorous technical review:

### What Do We Look For in a Counter-Report?

We analyze the document looking for cracks in its argumentation:

*   **Regulatory Errors:** Has the Building Technical Code (CTE) in force at the time of works been applied, or an obsolete standard?
*   **Methodological Flaws:** Was data collection correct? Were appropriate tools used? Were samplings sufficient?
*   **Calculation Inconsistencies:** Are loads well dimensioned? Are repair prices market-based?
*   **Bias and Subjectivity:** Has the expert issued personal opinions instead of objective technical conclusions?

### Value in Court

The counter-report not only serves to present an alternative view to the judge; it is a **strategic tool for your lawyer**. We prepare a technical script so they know exactly what to ask the opposing expert during cross-examination, exposing contradictions and weakening their credibility before the court.
