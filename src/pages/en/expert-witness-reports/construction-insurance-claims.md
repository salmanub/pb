## When the Incident is as Complex as the Project Itself

In the construction industry, a major incident—a fire, flood, partial collapse, or machinery failure—is not just a setback; it is a direct threat to the project's financial viability, deadlines, and reputation.

In this scenario, the insurance company will appoint a loss adjuster to defend its interests. **Who defends yours with the same level of technical rigor?**

Defending a claim under a **Construction All-Risk (CAR), Decennial, or Public Liability** policy requires a dual profile: an expert who not only masters insurance terminology but also has a deep understanding of engineering, construction processes, and damage valuation in civil and building works.

### Our Differentiating Factor: Civil Engineers who are also Insurance Experts

Our postgraduate degree as **Insurance Appraisers in Fire and Diverse Risks (IRD) from INESE** complements our core identity as **Chartered Civil Engineers**. This combination is your main strategic advantage:

* **We speak your language:** We understand structures, geotechnics, materials, and construction processes. We analyze the incident from its engineering root cause.
* **We speak the insurer's language:** We are familiar with the policies, exclusions, limits, and valuation criteria that companies apply.
* **We translate engineering into a claim:** We turn a complex technical problem into a solid and irrefutable argument for your claim, quantifying not only the obvious damage but also indirect costs, work stoppage, and loss of profit.

### Types of Construction Incidents Where We Add Value

Our expert report is your best asset for defending complex claims related to:

* **Construction All-Risk (CAR) Policies:** We assess material damage from fires, floods, weather events, design or execution errors, and their impact on the project schedule.
* **Decennial Insurance:** We intervene when structural damages that affect the building's stability appear within 10 years after project completion, preparing the technical report that substantiates the claim.
* **Public Liability (PL) Policies:** We defend the construction company against third-party claims for damages caused during the execution of the work (damage to adjacent properties, accidents, etc.).
* **Machinery Breakdown:** We assess failures and breakdowns in heavy machinery essential to the project, evaluating both the repair cost and the impact of its downtime.

A party-appointed expert report prepared by an engineer is not an expense; it is a **strategic investment to protect your project's margin and future.**
