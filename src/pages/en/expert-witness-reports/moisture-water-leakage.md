## Tired of Fighting Against Moisture?

Wall stains, mold smell, or leaks are problems that go beyond aesthetics. They affect your home's healthiness and can silently degrade the building's structure. Applying "anti-moisture" paints or superficial solutions without addressing the cause is a common mistake that only temporarily masks the problem.

For a definitive solution, a professional **technical diagnosis** that precisely determines the type and origin of moisture is essential.

### The Origin is Key: Capillarity, Condensation, or Leakage

Our team of experts analyzes the evidence to identify the real cause, which is usually one of these three:

* **Leakage Moisture:** Water penetrates from the outside through roofs, facades, joints, or pipe breaks. These are most common in garages, basements, and under-roof floors.
* **Condensation Moisture:** Occurs when interior water vapor condenses on cold surfaces (windows, poorly insulated walls, thermal bridges). It typically manifests as mold in corners and closets.
* **Capillary Moisture:** Water rises from the ground through the pores in walls and foundations. It's typical in ground floors and basements.

### What Does Our Moisture Diagnosis Report Include?

We produce a technical assessment that will help you understand the problem and make claims or repairs with guarantees. The report includes:

1. **Origin Identification:** Clear conclusions about the moisture cause, using tools like moisture meters and thermography if necessary.
2. **Damage Mapping:** Detailed description of affected areas and damage severity.
3. **Technical Solution Proposal:** We specify the appropriate treatment (waterproofing, insulation improvement, ventilation, etc.) to eradicate the problem at its root.
4. **Economic Assessment:** An estimate of repair costs, fundamental for insurance or property owners association claims.
