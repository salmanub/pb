## The Technical Expert for Your Industrial Assets

An industrial warehouse is not just a space; it is a production or logistics tool. Its correct condition is vital. Whether ending a lease contract, detecting construction problems, or planning a refurbishment, counting on a **specialized expert report** is fundamental to making correct decisions and protecting your investment.

Our approach combines civil engineering rigor with deep knowledge of industrial sector pathologies and regulations.

### Key Situations Where We Intervene

Our warehouse expert service is essential for:

* **Evaluating Post-Lease Damages:** We objectively document and quantify damages caused by the tenant exceeding normal wear and tear (broken pavements, structural damage, unauthorized removal of elements), as in our success cases.
* **Diagnosing Pavement Pathologies:** We analyze causes of cracks, subsidence, breakage, or delamination in concrete slabs, determining if due to poor design, deficient execution, or improper use.
* **Verifying Structural Safety:** We inspect the main structure (steel or concrete) for damage, corrosion, deformations, or non-compliance with the original project compromising stability.
* **Checking Fire Protection:** We audit the status of passive (fireproof mortars, fire strips) and active protection, verifying adequacy to Industrial Establishments Fire Safety Regulations (RSCIEI).
* **Detecting Illegal Constructions:** We identify mezzanines, extensions, or other modifications made without the corresponding technical project or municipal license.
* **Supporting Claims:** We provide the necessary technical evidence for claims against builders for hidden defects or poor workmanship in new builds or rehabilitations.

### A Decisive Report for Your Warehouse Management

We understand the economic and operational implications of warehouse problems. Therefore, our reports go beyond diagnosis:

* **Clarity:** We explain technical causes understandably.
* **Solutions:** We propose the most appropriate repair actions.
* **Valuation:** We economically quantify damages and repairs.
* **Forcefulness:** We create a solid document for negotiations or legal proceedings.
