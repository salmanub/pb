## The Science Behind Structural Failure

In construction litigation where the financial risk is very high, simple visual observation carries no real evidentiary weight. Our approach as civil engineers focuses on demonstrating irrefutably **why and how** the structural element failed.

To achieve this, we employ **Finite Element Analysis (FEA)** modeling, recreating the exact load conditions. For example, in the event of the collapse of a slender structure subjected to dynamic actions, we do not speculate; we calculate the peak wind pressure and the ultimate limit state of the sections involved.

Proving that stresses exceeded the material's elastic limit due to a design defect or poor execution is the difference between winning or losing a multimillion-dollar lawsuit.


## Independent Technical Validation Consulting

A fundamental pillar of our firm is the **technical audit and validation of structural repair systems**.

Frequently, construction companies, engineering firms, and property owners rely on the free prescription of large construction chemical manufacturers to solve serious pathologies (carbon fiber reinforcements, epoxy resins, micropiling systems). However, it is vital to understand that **a commercial technical service prescribes products but does not assume civil liability for the structural recalculation of your building**.

Our firm acts as the **independent safety link**:
1.  **Solution Audit:** We recalculate the repair project proposed by the manufacturer to verify its strict mathematical viability in your specific case.
2.  **Prescriptive Independence:** By not selling materials, we guarantee that the chosen solution is technically optimal, without commercial bias.
3.  **Assumption of Liability (Visa):** We assume the Civil Liability (RC) for the reinforcement or repair design, issuing a report certified by the Official College of Engineers.

Investing in this paid technical consulting is the only way to legally shield the structural safety of your asset and guarantee that the pathology will not reproduce in the future.
