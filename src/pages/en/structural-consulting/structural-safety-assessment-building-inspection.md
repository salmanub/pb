## Received a Negative ITE or IEE Report? Don't Panic, Take Action.

The Technical Building Inspection (ITE) is a legal requirement that certifies the state of conservation of your property. When the result is negative, the city council requires the property owner to submit an action plan to correct the deficiencies within a specified period.

This is where our role as structural experts is fundamental. We don't just confirm what the ITE report says; we go a step further to provide you with a **precise diagnosis and a viable action plan**.

### From "Serious Deficiency" to Concrete Solution

An ITE inspector might point out "Significant cracks in the basement floor beams," but the community needs to know:

* **Why have these cracks appeared?** Is it due to reinforcement corrosion, ground settlement, or excessive load?
* **What is the real risk?** Is there an imminent danger of collapse, or is it a slowly progressing problem?
* **What is the most effective and durable solution?** Is a simple seal enough, or is concrete repair or complex structural reinforcement needed?

Our structural safety assessment report answers these questions clearly and rigorously.

### Our Assessment Process

1.  **Analysis of the Prior Report:** We study the ITE or IEE report in detail to understand the critical points raised.
2.  **Detailed Structural Inspection:** We conduct a thorough visit to the property, focusing on the affected structural elements (foundations, columns, beams, slabs, load-bearing walls).
3.  **Root-Cause Diagnosis:** We apply our technical knowledge to determine the origin of the observed pathologies.
4.  **Technical Report with an Action Plan:** We deliver a report that includes the diagnosis, risk level, the proposed repair or reinforcement (describing the system to be used), and an estimation of action priorities.

This document is the tool the community needs to request quotes from specialized construction companies and make informed decisions to ensure the building's safety and future.
