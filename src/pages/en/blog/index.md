## Welcome to Our Technical Blog

In this space, we share **professional knowledge** about expert witness reports, building pathologies, claims, and technical aspects of civil engineering applied to the construction sector.

### What Will You Find in Our Blog?

* **Practical Guides:** How to identify problems, what to do when you discover them, and when you need an expert.
* **Technical Analysis:** Detailed explanations of construction pathologies and their solutions.
* **Legal Aspects:** Claim deadlines, consumer rights, and judicial procedures.
* **Real Cases:** Lessons learned from our success cases (anonymized).

### Our Commitment

We believe in spreading technical knowledge. Although some problems inevitably require professional intervention, many doubts can be resolved with clear and well-founded information.

**All our articles are written by chartered engineers** with extensive experience in judicial reports and technical opinions.
