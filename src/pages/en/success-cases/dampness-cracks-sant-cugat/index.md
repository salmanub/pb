## Intervention Methodology

### 1. On-Site Inspection
- Detailed visual assessment of pathologies
- Infrared thermography of walls
- Dampness measurements with hygrometer
- Analysis of the construction system

### 2. Documentation Study
- Review of the original project
- Analysis of previous interventions
- Evaluation of the insurance policy
- Verification of valid warranties

### 3. Specific Tests
The technical analysis identified:
- Defects in perimeter waterproofing
- Slight differential settlements
- Active leaks from the roof

### 4. Expert Documentation
Our report provided:
- Photographic and thermographic evidence
- Substantiated causal analysis
- Detailed economic valuation
- Technical repair proposals

## The Result: Comprehensive Repair Achieved

The expert report managed to:

1. Demonstrate the construction origin of the pathologies
2. Substantiate the claim to the ten-year insurance
3. Achieve approval for a comprehensive repair
4. Establish appropriate intervention protocols

> "The clarity of the report was key. The technical evidence and provided documentation were decisive for the insurance to assume the comprehensive repair."
>
> -- Homeowners, Sant Cugat del Vallès

### Case Impact

- **Repair valued at €45,000** covered by insurance
- **Definitive solution** to the leak problems
- Preventive **structural reinforcement** executed
- **Property appreciation** after the repairs

## Lessons Learned

This case exemplifies:

1. The importance of rigorous technical documentation
2. The value of specific instrumental tests
3. The effectiveness of a well-substantiated claim
4. The benefits of early intervention
