## Disagreements Over Payment Certificates? The Solution is an Objective Analysis

Payment certificates are documents that verify the amount of work completed in a period, serving as the basis for billing and payment by the property owner or developer. A disagreement at this point can freeze payments, stop work, and lead to costly litigation.

These conflicts typically arise from:

* **Measurement Errors:** Discrepancies between project measurements and what was actually executed.
* **Disputed Prices:** Items not defined in the contract that are valued at prices one party doesn't accept.
* **Poorly Executed Work:** The owner refuses to pay for items considered not meeting required quality standards.
* **Duplications or Non-existent Items:** Inclusion in the certification of work already paid for or not performed.

In this situation, one party's opinion against the other resolves nothing. An **expert, technical, and impartial third opinion** is needed.

### Our Payment Certificate Audit: Data, Not Opinions

Our service consists of performing a technical and economic "X-ray" of the dispute. The process is rigorous and focuses on evidence:

1. **Document Analysis:** We thoroughly study the construction contract, budget, specifications, project, and all certificates issued to date.
2. **On-site Inspection and Measurement:** We visit the site to measure and verify the disputed items on the ground. We check if the work is actually executed and if the measurements are correct.
3. **Review and Assessment:** We cross-reference actual site data with contractual documentation to identify and quantify each deviation, whether excess, deficient, or quality-related.

### The Expert Report: Your Tool to Unlock the Conflict

The result of our work is a clear and conclusive expert report that includes:

* A detailed **comparative analysis** between certified and actually measured work.
* **Photographic and technical documentation** proving our measurements.
* A **precise economic assessment** of the deviations found.
* Substantiated **technical conclusions** explaining the origin of the discrepancy.

This opinion serves as a powerful tool for negotiation, allowing parties to reach a fair agreement based on objective data. And if an agreement isn't possible, it becomes the fundamental expert evidence for mediation, arbitration, or legal proceedings.
