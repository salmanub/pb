## Are You Certain Your Investment is Being Built Correctly?

The execution project is one thing; the on-site reality is another. Pressures from deadlines and costs can lead to unauthorized material substitutions, deficient construction solutions, or incorrect interpretations of regulations. As a developer, investor, or final buyer, you need an external guarantee that the construction is faithful to the project you are paying for.

A **compliance audit report** is your best control tool to ensure the quality and legality of the work.

### What Do We Verify in Our Audit?

Our technical analysis focuses on the critical points that guarantee the project's viability and safety:

* **Compliance with the Technical Building Code (CTE):** We check that the solutions adopted on-site meet the basic requirements for structural safety (DB-SE), fire safety (DB-SI), health (DB-HS), noise protection (DB-HR), and energy savings (DB-HE).
* **Fidelity to the Execution Project:** We compare the geometry, materials, installations, and construction details executed with those defined in the reports, specifications, and plans of the approved project.
* **Quality of Materials:** We verify that the materials used have the corresponding CE markings and certificates and that their characteristics are as specified.
* **Correct Workmanship:** We analyze whether the execution of critical work units (structural joints, waterproofing, insulation) has been carried out following good construction practices.

### The Value of an Independent Report

Our audit report not only lists deviations but also documents them with photographs and provides a technical basis. This document gives you:

1.  **Legal Certainty:** A solid foundation to halt payments, demand corrections, or initiate a legal claim.
2.  **Investment Control:** It allows you to make informed decisions to protect your capital and the project's profitability.
3.  **Quality Assurance:** It ensures that the final product you receive or develop meets the expected standards of quality, safety, and comfort.
