The registration stamp — a hairline ring with curved mono text, a serif monogram and a reference line. Use sparingly as a credibility mark (colegiado nº, "ratificado"), as a favicon, or as a quiet watermark.

```jsx
<Seal monogram="VS" top="PERITO · BARCELONA" bottom="ECCAT · 16448" size={120} />
```

`tone`: `oxide` (default), `ink`, `light` (on dark). It is typographic only — no illustration.
