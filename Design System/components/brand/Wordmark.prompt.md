The denominative logo: serif "perito", an oxide seal dot, and mono "barcelona". Use `lockup` in headers, `stacked` (with descriptor) for footers/first contact, `mono` as a compact badge/favicon.

```jsx
<Wordmark variant="lockup" size={26} />
<Wordmark variant="stacked" tone="light" />
```

`tone="light"` for dark backgrounds.
